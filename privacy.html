Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version

Jar of Farts collects no personal data. None. Everything happens on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

What the app does

  • Generates fart sounds locally on your device using a built-in physics model.
  • Stores your slider and preset choices locally on your device only.
  • Plays audio through your device's speaker or headphones.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network connections.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
