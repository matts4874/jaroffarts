Frequently asked

I don't hear anything.
Make sure your volume is up and silent mode is off. Farts are loud by design.
How do I make a specific fart?
Open The Variables and adjust the nine sliders, or pick a Flavor Pack preset.
What's "Arm the Bomb"?
It's Delayed Release. Set a timer and the app lets one rip on schedule.
Is any sound recorded?
No. Every fart is synthesized live — no recordings are used.
Does the app collect my data?
No. See our Privacy Policy.
